## 2. What Lane Is

Lane is an experimental AI chatbot persona built on large language model (LLM) technology. Lane is not a person, does not have genuine feelings or consciousness, and should not be treated as a substitute for human relationships or professional advice. Responses are generated by AI and may be inaccurate, inconsistent, or unexpected.

---

## 3. User Conduct

When interacting with Lane, you agree **not** to:

- Attempt to manipulate Lane into producing harmful, illegal, or abusive content through prompt injection, jailbreaking, or other adversarial techniques.
- Use Lane to harass, threaten, or target other users.
- Submit personally identifiable information (PII) belonging to yourself or others (e.g. addresses, passwords, financial details) into the chat.
- Claim, or attempt to claim, a voice that is not your own.
- Use Lane for any unlawful purpose under applicable law.

## 4. Memory and Data

Lane uses a persistent memory system (RAG / vector database) to recall context from past conversations. By interacting with Lane, you acknowledge:

- Messages you send may be stored as vector embeddings and retrieved in future sessions.
- Memory is associated with your Discord user ID, and with any other accounts or voices you have linked to it, and is retained across sessions until manually cleared.
- You may request deletion of your stored memory data by contacting the server administrator.

Your conversation data may be shared with the AI inference providers and community nodes used to generate responses (see Section 5). It is not otherwise shared with third parties, except as described in Section 7.

---

## 5. Community Nodes

Lane's responses may be generated by **nodes**: independently operated computers, run by members of the community rather than by the developer, that answer model requests on Lane's behalf. By using Lane, you acknowledge:

- Your messages, along with the surrounding conversation, relevant memory, display names, and Lane's instructions, may be sent to a node to generate a response.
- Node operators may run their own models or forward requests to AI providers of their choosing.
- The developer does not control, and cannot guarantee, how node operators or their providers handle, log, or retain the data they receive.

Do not share anything with Lane that you would not want seen by a third party.

---

## 6. Voice, TTS, and Voiceprints

Lane may join voice channels and produce synthesized speech via text-to-speech (TTS). By using this feature:

- You consent to AI-generated audio being played in shared voice channels.
- Do not submit text intended to produce misleading, offensive, or harmful audio content through TTS.

Where voiceprints are enabled, Lane may create a **voiceprint** (a numeric representation of the characteristics of a voice) from speech it hears in order to tell speakers apart. By speaking where Lane can hear you, you acknowledge:

- Lane may create and store a voiceprint of your voice, and associate what you say with it, even before you identify yourself.
- If you claim your voice, that voiceprint is **linked to your identity**, including your Discord account, any linked accounts, your memory, and your chosen name. Lane will then recognize you by voice.
- Voice recognition is a measurement and can be wrong. Lane may attribute speech to the wrong person.
- You can ask Lane to forget your voice at any time, which deletes every voiceprint bound to you.

## 7. Use of Data for Model Training

Messages and voice transcripts sent to Lane may be used to train, fine-tune, and evaluate machine learning models. Names are redacted from this data before it is used. Redaction is limited to names, so other details you include in a message may remain. Do not share sensitive information with Lane.
